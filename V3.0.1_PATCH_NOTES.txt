PhotoUSBBackup v3.0.1
=====================
- Based directly on v3.0 Album Copy.
- Replaced FileManager.copyItem(localTemp -> USB .partial) with 1 MiB chunked streaming.
- Explicitly closes source/destination FileHandles on success and error paths.
- Verifies bytes written and USB .partial size before rename.
- Verifies final size after rename.
- Deletes incomplete .partial files after failures.
- Error log now shows stage + NSError domain/code/message.
- Album hierarchy, easy skip, PhotoKit local staging, one-at-a-time processing, bundle ID and workflow remain unchanged.
